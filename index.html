Our Programmes & Services

School Tuition

One-to-one academic support for all core subjects — covering the Kerala State syllabus and CBSE curriculum from KG to Plus Two.

Madrasa Tuition & Qur'an Class

Basic Foundation Class

Frequently Asked Questions

Ask Us on WhatsApp
What age groups do you accept?
We welcome students from Kindergarten (KG) right through to Plus Two (Class 12). Our youngest learners join for Basic Foundation Classes, while older students enrol for subject-specific one-to-one tuition. There is a programme suited to every stage of school education.
Which syllabus do you support?
We support both the Kerala State syllabus and the CBSE syllabus across all subjects and grades. Our teachers are well-versed in both curricula and align every session to the student's specific school board requirements.
Are online classes available?
Yes. We offer both online and offline (in-person) classes. Online sessions are conducted via live video call so students anywhere in Kerala can access personalised teaching from the comfort of their home. The same one-to-one model applies online.
How do I enrol my child?
It is easy. Fill in the enquiry form on this page or WhatsApp us on +91 85898 18284. We will then schedule a free demo class so your child can experience our teaching before any commitment is made.
What are the class timings?
Classes are available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM. Timings are fully flexible and are agreed upon based on the student's school schedule and the availability of the assigned teacher. We work around you.
